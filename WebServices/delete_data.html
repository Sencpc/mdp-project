Data Deletion Request

If you wish to delete specific data associated with your account for Mindful Life (developed by Institut Sains dan Teknologi Terpadu Surabaya) without deleting your entire account, you can do so directly within our application or by contacting our support team.

How to Request Data Deletion

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can manage and delete most of your data directly through the app interface:

  1. Habits: Navigate to the Habits section and delete individual habits you no longer wish to track.
  2. Chat History & AI Memory: Go to the AI Chat page/settings to clear your chat history or reset the AI's contextual memory of you.

Option 2: Email Request

If you want to request the deletion of other specific data points (such as sleep logs, nutrition logs, or specific profile details) without deleting your account:

  1. Send an email to our support team.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request
  3. Clearly specify which data you want deleted (e.g., "Please delete all my sleep logs from last year").
  4. Include your registered Username so we can accurately identify your records.
  5. We will securely delete the requested data within 7-14 business days and notify you once it's done.

Types of Data That Can Be Deleted

  • Individual Health & Fitness entries (Habits, Sleep Logs, Nutrition Logs).
  • Specific Personal Information fields (Weight, height, blood type, medical conditions).
  • Communication Data (AI Chat logs and memory).

Data Kept & Retention

Data that you do not explicitly delete or request to be deleted will remain securely stored to ensure the app continues to function properly for you. As outlined in our Privacy Policy, we may retain completely anonymized and aggregated data for analytical purposes.
