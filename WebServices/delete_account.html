Account Deletion Request

We value your privacy. If you no longer wish to use Mindful Life (developed by Institut Sains dan Teknologi Terpadu Surabaya) and would like to delete your account, follow the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to our support team from the email address associated with your account (or simply state your registered username).
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. In the body of the email, clearly state that you wish to delete your account and provide your Username to help us identify you.
  4. Our team will process your request and securely delete your data within 7-14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

Types of Data Deleted

Upon processing your account deletion request, we will permanently remove the following information from our active servers:

  • Personal Information: Name, username, birth date, and emergency contact details.
  • Health & Fitness Data: Weight, height, blood type, medical conditions, sleep logs, nutrition logs, and daily habits.
  • Communication Data: All saved chat logs and AI interactions.

Data Kept & Retention Periods

Please note the following exceptions regarding data retention:

  • Aggregated Data: We may retain certain anonymous, aggregated usage data that is completely decoupled from your personal identity for analytical purposes.
  • Legal Obligations: In rare cases, we may retain specific system logs for up to 90 days if required to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.
